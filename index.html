About

I'm a PhD student in Dr. Li's lab at Miami University, originally from Bangladesh. My work combines computational and wet-lab approaches to understand how genomes are built and how they change over evolutionary time.

On the computational side I build chromosome-level genome assemblies and run comparative and synteny analyses across many species. At the bench I do RNA extraction and FISH microscopy to look directly at the cells where insects and their symbionts meet.

Research

Insects

Insect–microbe symbiosis

Comparative genomics of aphids and other Hemiptera, and the bacteriocytes that house their obligate symbionts.

Genomes

Chromosome-level assembly

Assembling high-quality reference genomes and tracing gene order, synteny and genome rearrangement across lineages.

Plants

Plant polyploidy

Whole-genome duplication and subgenome evolution, focusing on the Hawaiian silversword alliance and other Asteraceae.
